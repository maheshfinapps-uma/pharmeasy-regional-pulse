# PharmaEasy Regional Pulse — One-Page Memo

## Title

Guntur Regional Sales Movement — April to June 2026

## Context

The April–June 2026 cleaned order dataset was validated through the SQL metrics pipeline and reviewed using an 8% operational movement threshold. [HIGH]

## Key Insight

Guntur sales increased from ₹62,442.27 in April to ₹138,738.93 in May, a +122.19% month-over-month movement, before declining to ₹99,745.18 in June (-28.11%). [HIGH]

## Evidence

The April-to-May movement is the largest-magnitude flagged regional movement in the validated analysis. The regional aggregate establishes the movement, but does not establish the underlying business cause or identify a single category as the cause. [HIGH]

## Recommendation

Review Guntur order-level and category-level detail for April–June before taking material operational action or assigning a causal explanation. [MEDIUM]

## Next Check

Reconcile Guntur order counts and category contributions and review available operational context before the next regional performance review. [MEDIUM]

## Assumptions

The analysis assumes that the cleaned order-level data accurately represents the April–June 2026 sales activity and that no unrecorded external business event materially explains the Guntur movement. [MEDIUM]
